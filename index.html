About

I am a master's student in mathematics at the Courant Institute (NYU), graduating May 2027. My master's thesis, advised by Yuri Tschinkel, is a computational study of combinatorial Burnside groups and classes of finite group actions in equivariant birational geometry. I work in computational algebraic geometry and numerical analysis, and I build mathematical software in Julia. Before NYU I completed a double degree in Mathematics and Systems & Computing Engineering at Universidad de los Andes (Colombia), advised by Mauricio Velasco in mathematics and by Nicolas Cardozo in Systems and Computer Engineering.

Research interests

Computational and numerical algebraic geometry; computer algebra; numerical analysis; equivariant and arithmetic geometry from a computational viewpoint; formal verification of algebraic and numerical computation.

Software and projects

Several of these packages were developed with AI coding assistants (Claude, Codex, Cursor) under my supervision; the READMEs of HomotopyGetsReal.jl and combinatorial-burnside-groups document this.

HomotopyGetsReal.jl

Code · Documentation

Julia package for the numerical decomposition of real algebraic curves and surfaces into cell complexes via homotopy continuation. Reimplements the Bertini_real critical-point pipeline on top of HomotopyContinuation.jl, with an optional generic-projection mode and an isosingular deflation diagnostic. Registered in Julia's General registry, latest version v0.2.2, MIT license, Documenter site, 538-test suite. A software paper is in preparation (not submitted).

M2Bridge.jl

Code

Prototype persistent Julia interface to Macaulay2 (one long-lived M2 process, exchanging code and results with a Julia session). Version 0.1.0, not registered.

combinatorial-burnside-groups

Code

Julia/OSCAR implementation of the combinatorial Burnside groups BC_n(G) of Tschinkel, Yang and Zhang (arXiv:2112.12801). Reproduces the 52 published values located in the paper and passes 88 validation checks (52 against the paper, 36 internal cross-checks), with Smith normal form certificates.

LPPCheck.jl

Code

Computational stress test, with Oscar.jl in exact arithmetic over Q, of the Eisenbud-Green-Harris and lex-plus-powers statements. About 2,100 checked instances with no violations; computational evidence only, not a proof.

Meshfree-GFDM-Algebraic-Varieties

Code

Numerical Methods II course project. Generalized finite differences for elliptic PDEs on curved and singular algebraic varieties, using homotopy continuation to locate singularities.

Lean 4 verification project

Course project (MATH-GA 2650): verification in Lean 4 of Smith normal form certificates produced by the combinatorial Burnside group computations. The repository is private.

Earlier work: Resolviendo ecuaciones polinomiales (Julia/Oscar code from my undergraduate thesis on solving polynomial systems). Forks of bertini_real and HomotopyContinuation.jl are also on my GitHub.

Research
